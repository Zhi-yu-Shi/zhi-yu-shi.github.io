Where does the energy of an electrical discharge go, and which reactions determine what it accomplishes? I study how a discharge can sustain a flame, change its transition to detonation, or transform a metal catalyst. Machine-learned molecular dynamics lets me examine the individual collisions and surface reactions behind these larger-scale processes.
